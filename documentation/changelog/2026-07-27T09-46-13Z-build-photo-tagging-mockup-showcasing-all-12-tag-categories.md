# Build photo-tagging mockup showcasing all 12 tag categories

Added `prototypes/mockup/` — a real clickable prototype (in-memory fake DB, same
convention as `upload-and-share-mockup/`) covering every category from
`documentation/tags/TAXONOMY.md`: bounding-box tagging with local-vs-linked-account
badges, the invite CTA, entity-owned relationship chaining with a 2-hop graph-walk
search, the privacy category forcing a tag private, and the blur-preview
share-as-album review. Requested to showcase tagging's design possibilities visually
before any of it is built. Cross-linked from `documentation/tags/README.md`.
