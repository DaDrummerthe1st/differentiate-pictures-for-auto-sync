# Capture v1/v2 NAS hardware roadmap and early-profitability notes

Joakim raised a hardware roadmap for the eventual sellable NAS/router device (v1: RPi3-class, already in use; v2: speculative custom-PCB, GPU-capable, hot-swappable-SSD, modular incl. 5G modem) plus a push to reach profitability early even while staying open source, via community feeling, the DFS "torrent-base," and Sweden's forced 3-year manufacturing guarantee. Researched market precedent (ZimaBoard, Turing Pi, Pine64, industrial-design impact on non-technical buyers, crowdfunding as an open-hardware funding path, Konsumentköplagen) and wrote it up as new/expanded docs so it isn't lost before V2/V3 of the rollout plan.
