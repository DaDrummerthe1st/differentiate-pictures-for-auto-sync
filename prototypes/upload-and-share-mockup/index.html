Upload & share — clickable mockup

Static local prototype of the design in documentation/upload-and-share/. No server, no persistence beyond this page load — every "sent"/"shared" state below is faked client-side in script.js.

Local file only — open directly in a browser, or python3 -m http.server from this folder. Never hosted externally.

Ownership model

Every uploader owns her own copy. Two terms, set per photo with a per-album default.

STRICT

Revocable viewing grant

A live permission check, not a copy

  • Resharing onward blocked without asking the owner
  • Download / export blocked
  • Revocable at any time
  • Enforced as a per-request check — one server, no new protocol
  • Tagging her own copy — always allowed
FREE

Full, irrevocable co-ownership

"Once shared free, it can't be taken back"

  • Resharing onward allowed, including further down the chain
  • Download / export allowed
  • Not revocable — a genuine independent owner
  • She sets her own terms for her own further shares
  • Tagging her own copy — always allowed
Moderation supersedes both tiers

A perceptual-hash blocklist removes flagged content unconditionally — no owner's or sharee's terms override a takedown. See OWNERSHIP.md.

Upload flow

A web upload has no source folder — the batch name is typed once and becomes an auto-created tag.
